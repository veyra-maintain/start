--- Page 1: Before you start ---
Step 1. Switch off the power supply with the rocker switch at the back of the case and unplug the power cable.
Step 2. Touch the bare metal of the case to discharge static electricity.
Step 3. Wipe the processor surface with isopropyl alcohol and a lint-free cloth.
--- Page 2: Installing the cooler ---
Step 4. Peel the protective plastic film off the base of the cooler.
Step 5. Squeeze one pea-sized drop of thermal paste onto the centre of the processor. Do not spread it; the cooler's pressure will do that.
Step 6. Lower the cooler straight down onto the processor, aligning the four screws with the four mounting holes. Do not slide it.
Step 7. Tighten the four screws in a diagonal pattern: top-left, bottom-right, top-right, bottom-left. Give each screw two turns at a time and repeat until all four are snug. Never fully tighten one screw before the others.
Step 8. Plug the cooler fan cable into the CPU_FAN header.
--- Page 3: Check ---
Step 9. Plug the power cable in and switch the power supply on.
Step 10. Confirm that the fan spins and the processor temperature is below 60 C at idle.
